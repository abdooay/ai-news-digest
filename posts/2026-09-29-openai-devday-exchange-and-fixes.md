## What changed

**DevDay Exchange.** Alongside its main DevDay event in San Francisco (September 29, 2026), OpenAI announced "DevDay Exchange" — a series of in-person meetups for developers in eight cities, starting in October: Bengaluru (Oct 16), Tokyo (Oct 20), Seoul (Oct 22), Berlin (Oct 26), Paris (Oct 28), London (Nov 3), São Paulo (Nov 6), and Mexico City (Nov 11). OpenAI describes them as a chance to "swap build notes, share real projects, and meet the teams building OpenAI tools."

**Image encoding fix.** On September 25, 2026, OpenAI fixed a bug in GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna (its two newest models, released September 22) that was degrading how well the models understood images. OpenAI says this improves results on visual tasks, including using a computer's screen.

## Why it matters

The image fix is worth knowing about if you use GPT-6 Sol or Luna for anything involving pictures or screenshots — including "computer use" tasks, where the model looks at a screen to decide what to click. Results from before September 25 may have been worse than the models are actually capable of.

The DevDay Exchange events are a way to get hands-on time with OpenAI's teams and other builders without traveling to San Francisco, if one of the eight cities is near you.

## How to use it

- If you rely on image understanding with GPT-6 Sol or GPT-6 Luna, OpenAI recommends re-running your evaluations and retrying any workflows that seemed to perform poorly on images before September 25 — the fix may have already resolved the issue.
- Applications for DevDay Exchange events are handled at events.openai.com; check the specific city page for that event's deadline, since OpenAI noted limited space.
