## What changed

On September 22, 2026, OpenAI released two new models, GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, built using the same methods as GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI's top-tier model launched earlier). OpenAI says both models improve on their GPT-5.6 predecessors in professional work, factual accuracy, coding, and computer use.

The two models target different jobs:
- **GPT-6 Sol** is meant for everyday professional and technical work, including coding, and has more reasoning capability.
- **GPT-6 Luna** is a cheaper, faster model meant for high-volume tasks with a clear goal — summarizing documents, extracting information, or answering quick questions.

OpenAI says GPT-6 Sol "makes about half as many mistakes" as GPT-5.6 Sol, reaching reliability close to the larger Astra model at much lower cost. On independent benchmark tests reported after launch: GPT-6 Sol scored 68.8% on DeepSWE 1.1 (a coding benchmark) at its highest reasoning setting, 60.5% on OSWorld 2.0 (a computer-interface test), 56.4% on Agents' Last Exam (long, multi-step professional tasks), and 33.2% on AutomationBench 1.0.6 (completing workflows across many tools) at a reported cost of $0.27 per task. GPT-6 Luna scored 66.6% on DeepSWE 1.1.

## Why it matters

Price is the headline change. GPT-6 Sol costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — half of what GPT-5.6 Sol cost during its promotional pricing period, and that promotional-level price is now the standard, ongoing rate. GPT-6 Luna costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, down from $0.20 and $1.20. For anyone running high-volume workloads — the kind of use case Luna targets — that's a meaningful, permanent cost reduction rather than a temporary discount.

Combined with the accuracy improvement on Sol, this is aimed at making mid-tier and cheap-tier models good enough for more production use, instead of requiring the most expensive model for reliability.

## How to use it

- Both models are available now through the OpenAI API.
- They're also rolling out in ChatGPT Work and in Codex (OpenAI's coding tool) for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans.
- Free and Go (a lower ChatGPT tier) users can try GPT-6 Luna through the desktop app.
- Rollout to ChatGPT-based products happens gradually over the course of the launch day, so it may not appear for every account immediately.
