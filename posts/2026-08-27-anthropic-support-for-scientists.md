## What changed

Anthropic announced three things aimed at academic and nonprofit researchers:

1. **Claude Team Plan for Scientists**: 10,000 seats worldwide, free at the standard tier, with a discounted premium tier ($15/month, 5x the usual usage limits) for one year. To qualify, you need to be a principal investigator (the lead researcher on a study) or equivalent, at an academic or nonprofit institution.
2. **Expanded AI for Science program**: previously focused on biological sciences, it now covers other research fields too. Eligible scientists can apply for up to $50,000 in Claude usage credits per project.
3. **Claude Science**: a product Anthropic first launched in June 2026 that connects to tools researchers already use, produces auditable output (a record of what was done and how), and gives flexible access to computing resources.

One restriction stays in place: biology and chemistry researchers are limited to Claude's Opus-class models, and the regular Fable models still refuse professional biology and drug-development questions, because of dual-use risk — the same knowledge that helps legitimate research could also help someone cause harm. Anthropic is separately working with the US government on a program that gives life-sciences professionals access to Mythos-class models (which have those restrictions eased for vetted users); Anthropic says the first participants are already enrolled.

## Why it matters

For academic researchers, the biggest practical change is cost: a free or heavily discounted Claude Team seat, plus up to $50,000 in project credits, removes a real budget barrier — especially for early-career researchers or labs without large grants. Widening the credit program beyond biology means researchers in physics, math, materials science, and other fields can now apply too.

## How to use it

- Principal investigators (or equivalent) at academic or nonprofit institutions can apply for a free or discounted Claude Team seat.
- Apply to the AI for Science program for up to $50,000 in project credits — now open to fields beyond biology.
- Claude Science is available now as Anthropic's dedicated research product.
