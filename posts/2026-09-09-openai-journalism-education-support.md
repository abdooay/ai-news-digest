## What changed

OpenAI announced ["Supporting journalism from classrooms to newsrooms"](https://openai.com/index/supporting-journalism-from-classrooms-to-newsrooms/) on September 8, 2026, describing a set of programs aimed at journalism education and news organizations.

For the 2026-2027 school year, OpenAI is giving CUNY's Newmark Journalism School and Northwestern's Medill School more than 400 free ChatGPT Edu subscriptions for graduate students and faculty. The goal, according to OpenAI, is to train future journalists to use AI "thoughtfully, creatively, ethically and responsibly." CUNY's Tow-Knight Center for Journalism Futures and Medill's Knight Lab are involved as academic partners.

OpenAI is also continuing several existing partnerships with news organizations:
- **American Journalism Project**: more than 50 news organizations get access to ChatGPT enterprise accounts and API credits, plus tools built by a joint "Product & AI Studio."
- **Lenfest Institute**: continued funding for an AI Collaborative and a fellowship program that places AI engineers at local news organizations.
- **WAN-IFRA** (a global news industry association): a "Newsroom AI Catalyst" program giving training, technical help, and API credits to more than 165 newsrooms worldwide.
- **INMA** (International News Media Association): support for regional discussions and webinars on AI in news organizations internationally.

## Why it matters

Local and international newsrooms often lack the budget or in-house expertise to adopt AI tools carefully, so free access and funded training can materially change what's possible for them. On the education side, this is OpenAI positioning itself inside journalism schools' curricula early, which shapes how the next generation of journalists is trained to use (or scrutinize) AI tools — worth being aware of if you follow debates about AI's role in news production and media literacy.

## How to use it

This is a set of institutional partnerships, not a consumer feature — there's nothing to turn on. If you're a student, faculty member, or staffer at one of the named institutions or programs, contact your institution directly for access details, since OpenAI's announcement doesn't include an application process for individuals.
