## What changed

OpenAI said its advertising business inside ChatGPT reached a **$1 billion annualized revenue run rate** in under 200 days since launch. A "run rate" means current monthly or quarterly revenue multiplied out to a full year — it's a projection based on recent activity, not money already collected for the year.

Alongside that milestone, OpenAI is expanding **self-service Ads Manager** — the tool that lets a business sign up, add payment details, set a budget, upload ads, and launch a campaign directly, without going through an ad agency — to advertisers in **India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa**. Ads Manager first launched in May 2026.

Other details from the announcement:
- ChatGPT ads are used by tens of thousands of advertisers already, and are available in more than 40 countries through OpenAI's Ads Solutions team, agencies, and technology partners.
- The platform supports cost-per-click (CPC) and outcome-based bidding, tracking of clicks and conversions, product feeds, targeting by location and platform, and custom audiences.
- OpenAI has partnered with more than 50 technology and measurement companies for ad tracking and reporting.
- Ads in ChatGPT are labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT's answers; OpenAI says advertising doesn't influence the answers ChatGPT gives.
- OpenAI cites results from partners: one e-commerce company reported a 3x return on ad spend over 28 days, and one partner said more than 80% of ad-driven ChatGPT traffic came from new customers.

## Why it matters

For businesses that want to advertise in ChatGPT, self-service access means they no longer need an agency or a direct sales relationship with OpenAI to get started — they can sign up and run a campaign themselves, similar to how self-service ad platforms work on other networks. The expansion specifically opens this up to smaller businesses and startups in the newly added regions, not just large advertisers.

## How to use it

- Businesses in India, Europe, the Middle East, or North Africa can sign up for ChatGPT's self-service Ads Manager to create and manage ad campaigns directly.
- Setup involves registering as an advertiser, adding payment information, and setting a budget and bids before launching a campaign.
- This is separate from ChatGPT's regular product features — it does not require any change for people just using ChatGPT normally.
