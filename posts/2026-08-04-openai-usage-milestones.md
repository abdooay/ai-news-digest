## What changed

On July 31, 2026, OpenAI published a post titled "Building Abundant Intelligence" that shared several usage figures for the first time:

- OpenAI's models now reach **more than one billion active users** and **more than two million businesses**.
- Six months after signing up, people send roughly **50% more messages each day** and use ChatGPT for about **twice as many kinds of work** compared to when they started.
- **Agentic work through Codex** (OpenAI's coding tool) now accounts for **99.8% of its weekly output tokens** — meaning almost all of the text Codex generates now comes from it working autonomously on tasks, rather than answering one-off questions. OpenAI names its own Finance team as one that has made these agentic tools a central part of its work.

The same post restates pricing and efficiency changes OpenAI announced earlier in the week: the 80%/20% price cuts to GPT-5.6 Luna and Terra, the new Fast mode for GPT-5.6 Sol, and efficiency gains behind those cuts (see our earlier post on the price changes for details).

## Why it matters

These are the first specific adoption numbers OpenAI has given publicly at this scale. The billion-plus active user and two-million-business figures show how widely ChatGPT and the API are used. The Codex figure — 99.8% of weekly output tokens going to agentic work — is a signal that, at least among Codex's existing user base, autonomous multi-step coding work has largely replaced simple one-shot code generation as the dominant way people use the tool.

## How to use it

This is a usage report, not a product change — there's no setting or API to act on. If you build products on top of ChatGPT or the API, the growing-usage and rising-message-volume figures are a signal that user engagement with conversational AI tends to deepen over time rather than plateau after initial signup.
