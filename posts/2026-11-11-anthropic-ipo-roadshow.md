## What changed

Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, started the "roadshow" for its planned initial public offering (IPO). An IPO is when a private company sells shares of itself to the public for the first time, so anyone can buy a piece of the company on a stock exchange. A roadshow is the series of investor meetings a company holds just before that happens, to explain the business and gauge interest.

The key numbers from the announcement:

- Anthropic plans to offer 825,000,000 Class A shares in total.
- Of those, 801,351,354 shares are being sold by Anthropic itself, and 23,648,646 are being sold by existing shareholders.
- Anthropic expects the price to land between $70.00 and $78.00 per share.
- Underwriters (the banks running the sale) have a 30-day option to buy up to 123,750,000 more shares.
- Anthropic applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol "ANT."
- The deal is being led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.

Anthropic won't get any money from the shares that existing shareholders are selling — only from the shares it sells itself.

## Why it matters

This is the clearest sign yet that Anthropic is close to becoming a publicly traded company. Once listed, Anthropic's finances become public and its stock price becomes a running public verdict on how well the company (and Claude) is doing. For users and developers, a successful IPO gives Anthropic a large pool of new cash, which can fund more compute, research, and product work — but it also means Anthropic will answer to public shareholders going forward, not just private investors.

Nothing about using Claude or the Claude API changes today because of this news.

## How to use it

There's no product or API change to act on here. If you're specifically interested in the financial details, note that as of this announcement the registration statement has been filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) but is **not yet effective** — meaning shares cannot legally be sold yet, and no one can accept an offer to buy them until the SEC approves the filing. Anthropic's own announcement explicitly says it isn't an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy securities.
