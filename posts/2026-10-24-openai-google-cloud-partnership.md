## What changed

OpenAI and Google Cloud announced a partnership on October 21, 2026, to bring OpenAI's frontier models and Codex (OpenAI's coding agent) agentic workflows into Google Cloud's Model Garden, a catalog of AI models Google Cloud customers can deploy. OpenAI models are also expected to become available soon in Google Antigravity and the Gemini Agent, two of Google's own developer and agent products.

## Why it matters

This lets enterprise and regulated-industry customers who already run on Google Cloud use OpenAI's models through Google's infrastructure, data governance, and access controls, instead of connecting to OpenAI's API directly. For companies with strict requirements about where data goes and who can access it, that can make it easier to adopt OpenAI's models without a separate vendor relationship. It also gives Google Cloud customers more model choice inside tools they already use.

## How to use it

Access will be through Google Cloud's Model Garden once the integration rolls out. OpenAI and Google have not given a specific availability date, and details on pricing and supported regions have not been published yet. Availability in Google Antigravity and the Gemini Agent is described only as "coming soon." Check Google Cloud's Model Garden and OpenAI's own announcement for rollout updates.
