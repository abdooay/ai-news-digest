## What changed

On July 27, 2026, Anthropic and Cognizant, a large IT and consulting services firm, announced an expanded partnership to integrate Claude into enterprise work across industries like manufacturing, life sciences, and insurance.

On the internal side, Cognizant says over 30,000 of its own employees have completed Claude training, and the company is building Claude into its internal platforms, including Flowsource (its software delivery platform), Neuro AI Engineering, and Neuro IT Ops. Claude Code specifically has been added to a "Spec-Driven Development" module inside Flowsource to help guide engineering work.

On the client side, Cognizant lists work it has already delivered using Claude:
- A customer experience portal for a manufacturer, built in six months.
- A contract-intelligence system for a biopharmaceutical client that cut contract review time by up to 40%.
- A risk-navigation tool that lets insurance underwriters complete evaluations in minutes instead of hours.

As part of the announcement, Cognizant becomes a "Global Premier Partner" in the Claude Partner Network (Anthropic's program for consulting and technology partners), and is rolling out a "Frontier Certified" program to grow a Claude-certified workforce.

## Why it matters

This is a business and go-to-market announcement rather than a technical one, but it's a signal of how Anthropic is trying to get Claude into large, non-tech companies: through consulting firms that already have relationships and industry expertise in areas like manufacturing and insurance, rather than selling directly. Cognizant's CEO framed it as bridging the gap between how fast AI capability is improving and how slowly large enterprises can actually absorb it. If your company works with Cognizant or a similar consulting partner, this is a sign Claude-based projects may show up in those engagements.

## How to use it

There's no product or API change to adopt here. If you work at a company that partners with Cognizant, the practical next step would be to ask your Cognizant account team about their Claude-based offerings (Flowsource, Neuro AI Engineering, Neuro IT Ops) if you're interested.
