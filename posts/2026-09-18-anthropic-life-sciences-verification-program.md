## What changed

Anthropic launched the Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP). It gives verified life-science organizations — academic labs, startups, pharmaceutical companies — access to Claude's Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet models with a "refined set of safeguards" that are more permissive for biology-related work, such as drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, and manufacturing.

Normally Claude blocks a lot of detailed biology requests by default, because it's hard for an automated safeguard to tell a legitimate researcher apart from someone trying to cause harm. This program is Anthropic's way of solving that: instead of blocking requests in real time, verified organizations get "offline monitoring" — usage patterns are reviewed after the fact against the use case the organization declared, rather than every message being checked live. Other safeguards, like ones aimed at cybersecurity misuse, stay in place regardless.

There are two grant types:
- **Standard Use**: covers most day-to-day life-science work, can apply to a whole team, and is renewed once a year.
- **High-risk Use**: for more sensitive, specialized work, applies to a single research project rather than a whole team, and must be renewed every six months.

To get in, an organization applies and goes through a verification process that checks its research credentials, security standards, and ethical research oversight. Anthropic said it expects to enroll hundreds of organizations in the program's first week.

## Why it matters

Biology research often needs to discuss things — pathogens, toxins, lab procedures — that look identical whether the person asking is a legitimate scientist or someone trying to misuse the information. Broad, automatic safety filters tend to either block real researchers or let through requests they shouldn't. This program tries to fix that specific tension for a vetted group, without loosening safety measures for everyone else. For life-science teams that have been running into Claude's default biology restrictions, this is a path to get that friction removed.

## How to use it

Life-science organizations interested in verified access should apply through Anthropic's application form (linked on the announcement page). Expect the review to look at your organization's credentials, security practices, and ethical oversight process. Standard Use grants renew yearly; High-risk Use grants renew every six months and need extra vetting.
