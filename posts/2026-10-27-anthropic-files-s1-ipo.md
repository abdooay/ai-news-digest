## What changed

Anthropic, PBC filed a Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on October 26, 2026. A Form S-1 is the paperwork a US company must file before it can sell shares to the public for the first time — this step is usually called an "initial public offering," or IPO.

Key facts from the filing announcement:

- Anthropic plans to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol **"ANT."**
- The number of shares to be offered, and the price range for those shares, have **not** been set yet.
- Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC are named as the banks to contact for a preliminary prospectus (a document with more financial detail) once one is available.
- The registration statement has been filed with the SEC but is **not yet effective**. Until it is, no shares can legally be sold, and no offers to buy them can be accepted.

Anthropic describes itself as a Public Benefit Corporation — a type of company that is legally required to balance shareholder profit with a stated public-benefit mission, which for Anthropic is "to ensure the world safely makes the transition through transformative AI."

The announcement gives no financial figures (like revenue, valuation, or how much money the IPO aims to raise) and includes no executive quotes.

## Why it matters

This is the first public confirmation that Anthropic, the company behind the Claude models, intends to become a publicly traded company. For developers and businesses using Claude, an IPO itself does not change products or pricing on its own. But going public means Anthropic will eventually have to publish detailed financial reports on a regular schedule, which is new — until now, Anthropic's finances have been largely private.

It's worth being precise about what today's news is, and isn't: filing an S-1 is a required step before an IPO, not the IPO itself. The company still has to set a share price and number of shares, get SEC approval ("effectiveness"), and then actually sell shares on an exchange. Any of those steps could still change the timeline.

## How to use it

There's no action required from developers or API users right now — nothing about the Claude API, Claude Code, or pricing has changed because of this filing. If you're interested in following the process:

- Watch for the "effective" registration and a set share price/count, which typically come shortly before the stock starts trading.
- The preliminary prospectus, once released, will contain Anthropic's first detailed public financial disclosures — worth reading if you want insight into the company's revenue and growth.
