## What changed

On October 8, 2026, at the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy's "Science: A New Golden Age" summit in Washington, DC, Anthropic announced a **$150 million commitment over three years** to the **Genesis Mission** — a US federal initiative to speed up scientific and technological discovery using AI.

Specifically, Anthropic says it will:

- Make Claude available to more than 15 Genesis Mission agencies, including NASA, the National Institutes of Health, and the National Science Foundation.
- Give Claude, Claude Code, and API credits to several hundred Genesis Mission research projects.
- Partner with agencies and national laboratories on priorities including fusion energy and quantum computing.
- Provide training and onboarding support to scientists, and help agencies that are new to the Genesis Mission launch their first projects.

This adds to work Anthropic already had running: a partnership with the US Department of Energy announced last December that brought Claude to national laboratory scientists; "Claude Science," a research workbench launched earlier in 2026 that integrates common research tools; 10,000 free or discounted Claude seats for academic scientists; an "AI for Science" program giving credits to researchers on high-impact projects; and a research preview of the "Model Hardware Standard," a shared specification for letting AI agents safely operate lab instruments.

## Why it matters

This is Anthropic positioning Claude as infrastructure for US government-funded science, not just a product scientists buy on their own. If you're a researcher at one of the listed agencies or national laboratories, this is a route to get Claude access, credits, and support that didn't exist before. The fusion energy and quantum computing partnerships are notable because those are areas where AI-assisted research is still fairly new and the payoff (if real) could be large.

## How to use it

- Researchers at Genesis Mission agencies (NASA, NIH, NSF, and others) should watch for announcements from their own agency about Claude access and credits, since Anthropic says onboarding will be done agency-by-agency.
- Academic scientists can also look into Anthropic's separate "AI for Science" credits program and the free/discounted Claude seats program mentioned on the announcement page.
