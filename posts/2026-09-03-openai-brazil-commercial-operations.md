## What changed

On August 27, 2026, OpenAI launched commercial operations in Brazil, opening an office in São Paulo. This is OpenAI's first office in the Americas outside the United States.

Until now, OpenAI's products were used in Brazil informally, without a local sales presence. The new office lets Brazilian companies buy OpenAI's services (like ChatGPT Enterprise and API access) through a local team instead of dealing only with OpenAI's US operations.

Key numbers OpenAI shared:
- Brazil is ChatGPT's third-largest market by weekly active users, with about 215 million messages sent there every day.
- Brazil's ChatGPT user base has nearly doubled over the past year.
- 35% of individual ChatGPT messages in Brazil are work-related, compared with a 30% global average.
- Brazil ranks second worldwide for developers using the OpenAI API. Use of Codex (OpenAI's coding tool) in Brazil grew more than elevenfold since early 2026.
- ChatGPT Enterprise seats in Brazil grew fivefold year over year.
- An OpenAI-funded study by RegLab estimates AI could add nearly R$1 trillion (about US$194 billion) to Brazil's economy by 2030.

OpenAI also announced local partnerships: ChatGPT Edu accounts with reasoning-model credits for the ITA engineering school, an AI research collaboration with the IMPA mathematics institute, a clinical-information prototype with Hospital das Clínicas, and a cooperation agreement with the São Paulo city government (via its IT company, Prodam).

## Why it matters

This is a business and infrastructure move, not a new product. If you use OpenAI's products in Brazil, it means local sales support and, likely, more localized partnerships and pricing options going forward. For developers, it signals continued investment in the market — Brazil is already OpenAI's second-largest developer market by API usage, so this office is meant to support that growth rather than start it.

## How to use it

There is no new feature to turn on here. Brazilian businesses interested in OpenAI's enterprise or API offerings can now go through a local sales channel instead of only the US-based one.
