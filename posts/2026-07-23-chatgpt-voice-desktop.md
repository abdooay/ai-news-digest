## What changed

OpenAI rolled out ChatGPT Voice in the ChatGPT desktop app for macOS and Windows. It lets you control your computer and direct multiple agents running in ChatGPT Work or Codex (OpenAI's coding agent) using just your voice. It is powered by GPT-Live, OpenAI's voice model family, so it can speak, listen, and coordinate work in the app at the same time, rather than waiting for you to finish talking before responding.

The rollout covers the Plus, Pro, Business, Edu, and Enterprise ChatGPT plans.

## Why it matters

Before this, directing ChatGPT Work or Codex meant typing instructions. Voice control removes that step, so you can describe a multi-step task — like creating a task thread, or checking on an agent's progress — out loud while doing something else on your computer. Because the underlying model can listen and speak at the same time, it can also be interrupted or redirected mid-task, closer to how you'd manage a task with a person.

## How to use it

- Open the ChatGPT desktop app on macOS or Windows.
- Switch to Work or Codex.
- Use the voice control to speak your instructions instead of typing them.
- Requires a Plus, Pro, Business, Edu, or Enterprise plan.
