## What changed

On September 10, 2026, OpenAI introduced **ChatGPT for Financial Services**, a version of ChatGPT Work aimed at investment banking and equity research, developed with design partners Morgan Stanley and Evercore. It combines built-in financial data with GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI's latest model) to help teams do research, build financial models, and produce client-ready documents.

Key features:
- **Built-in premium data.** The product includes licensed datasets from providers like Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News, and Crunchbase — covering earnings transcripts, financial statements, company fundamentals, and private-company data — hosted and indexed directly by OpenAI, so teams don't need to set up separate contracts or data connectors.
- **Traceable citations.** Figures and claims can be traced back to the specific source document or table they came from, so analysts can check the evidence behind a number as they build on it.
- **Access to existing subscriptions.** OpenAI is working with S&P Capital IQ, LSEG, MSCI, Dow Jones Factiva, and Moody's so that a firm's existing data subscriptions can be recognized automatically through a user's ChatGPT sign-in, rather than requiring separate logins.
- **Firm templates.** Administrators can publish Excel, Word, and PowerPoint templates so that analysis gets turned into valuation models, research notes, and pitch decks in the firm's own format and style.
- **Enterprise security controls**, including SAML single sign-on, SCIM user provisioning, and role-based access — the same controls ChatGPT Enterprise already offers — plus workspace-level information barriers for firms that need to keep certain teams' data separated.
- **Benchmark result.** On an internal test called OfficeQA Pro, which checks whether a model can find and analyze information across U.S. Treasury Bulletins (including complex tables and footnotes), GPT-6 Astra scored 69.9%, compared with 60.2% for the previous GPT-5.6 Sol model.

## Why it matters

Financial analysts often lose significant time just locating and verifying the right numbers before they can even start real analysis — chasing down data across multiple paid subscriptions, cross-checking figures by hand, and reformatting results into a firm's required templates. By hosting licensed data directly and making every figure traceable to its source, OpenAI is targeting that overhead specifically, rather than just offering a general-purpose chat assistant pointed at financial questions. The involvement of large financial institutions as design partners also suggests this is aimed at teams handling non-public or highly confidential data, where the built-in security controls and information barriers matter as much as the analysis itself.

## How to use it

- ChatGPT for Financial Services is available to eligible financial institutions; interested firms should contact OpenAI or their existing account team.
- It starts with investment banking and equity research use cases, based on the initial design partnership, with OpenAI planning to expand into other financial services categories over time.
- Firms with existing data subscriptions from partners like S&P Capital IQ or LSEG can connect them through their ChatGPT sign-in rather than separate logins.
