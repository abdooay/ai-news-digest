## What changed

OpenAI published ["The Work Now Within Reach"](https://openai.com/index/the-work-now-within-reach/) on September 8, 2026, a broad strategy update rather than a single product announcement. Key points:

- OpenAI says its products now reach more than one billion weekly active users and 2.5 million businesses.
- GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI's newest model, announced September 3 and covered in our earlier posts) is described as state-of-the-art on computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work.
- Inside OpenAI's own research organization, agents now do the equivalent of 3.1 "agent-workdays" of work for every one workday a human researcher puts in. (This matches the numbers in OpenAI's separate "Research acceleration" report from September 6, which we covered previously.)
- OpenAI describes a "full-stack" compute strategy — controlling data centers, chips, software, models, and products together, rather than relying only on off-the-shelf hardware. As part of this, OpenAI says its GPT-5.6 Sol model cut end-to-end serving costs by 20% and improved token-generation efficiency by more than 15%.
- OpenAI announced Jalapeño, its first custom inference chip (a chip designed specifically to run trained AI models efficiently, as opposed to training them). OpenAI claims it delivers 1.5 to 1.9 times the peak token throughput per watt of commercial systems it tested against.
- The post also references the Navier–Stokes math proof announced the same day (covered in our separate post) as an example of what OpenAI's systems can attempt.

## Why it matters

This is OpenAI explaining its overall strategy rather than shipping something you can use today. The user and business numbers (over 1 billion weekly users, 2.5 million businesses) give a sense of scale for anyone building on top of OpenAI's API or comparing platforms. The Jalapeño chip and the "full-stack" framing signal that OpenAI is trying to control its own hardware costs and supply, which — if it works — could affect API pricing and capacity over time, though OpenAI didn't give a timeline for when Jalapeño-based capacity would be broadly available.

## How to use it
