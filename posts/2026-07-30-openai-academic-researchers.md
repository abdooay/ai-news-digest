## What changed

OpenAI launched "ChatGPT for Academic Researchers," a program giving scientists, mathematicians, and engineers free access to its frontier models, including the GPT-5.6 family. It starts with 10,000 researchers and aims to grow to 100,000 by the end of 2027.

Each participant gets a dedicated workspace with business-grade privacy and security protections, and by default their data is not used to train OpenAI's models. Researchers can invite up to four collaborators into their workspace at no charge. OpenAI is also offering training and hands-on support, plus the chance to connect with other researchers in the program.

The stated goal is to help researchers with tasks like preparing grant applications and testing hypotheses.

## Why it matters

"Frontier models" means OpenAI's most capable, most recently released AI models — normally the most expensive tier to use heavily. This program removes the cost barrier for eligible academics, which matters if your research budget can't otherwise cover frontier-model usage. Because it's a limited, invite-based rollout (10,000 spots initially, not open to everyone), most researchers won't get in immediately, but the pool is set to grow steadily through 2027.

## How to use it

- Apply through OpenAI's application page at [openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-academic-researchers](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-academic-researchers/).
- If accepted, you can invite up to four colleagues from your institution into the same workspace at no cost.
