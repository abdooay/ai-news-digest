## What changed

Anthropic announced that Christopher Stubbs, an experimental physicist, has joined its Long-Term Benefit Trust. He is the Trust's first physicist member.

Some background on Stubbs: he was Dean of Science at Harvard from 2018 to 2024 and now holds a professorship in physics and astronomy there. In 1998, as part of the High-Z Supernova Search Team, he helped discover that the universe's expansion is speeding up — work that later won the team a Breakthrough Prize. He was also the first project scientist for the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, a telescope that began a 10-year survey of the southern sky in June 2026. He's a member of JASON, a group of scientists that has advised the US government on defense and technology since 1960.

The other trustees named in the announcement are Neil Buddy Shah (the Trust's chair), national security expert Richard Fontaine, and former Federal Reserve Chair Ben Bernanke.

## Why it matters

Anthropic is structured as a Public Benefit Corporation, a type of company that is legally required to balance making money with pursuing a stated public-good mission — in Anthropic's case, developing AI safely. The Long-Term Benefit Trust is a body set up to help enforce that balance: it can appoint a majority of Anthropic's board, and its trustees — who are independent of Anthropic's management and investors — advise the board on major decisions, especially ones involving AI's risks to society.

Adding a working scientist to that oversight body is notable because most of the Trust's prior public members have backgrounds in policy, finance, or national security rather than hands-on science. Stubbs's own supernova research is a case study in following data against the conventional wisdom of the time, which is the kind of judgment the Trust is meant to bring to decisions about a technology still being understood.

## How to use it

This is a governance and leadership update, not a product change — there's nothing to install or configure. If you want to understand how Anthropic's oversight structure works in more detail, Anthropic's announcement also mentions it has published its first Public Benefit Report, which tracks the company's progress against its charter.
