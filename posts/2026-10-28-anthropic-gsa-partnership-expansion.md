## What changed

Anthropic announced an expanded partnership with the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA), published October 27, 2026. The new agreement gives government customers a 40% discount across every Claude product:

- **Broader discount scope**: the 40% discount now applies to Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API — all product surfaces, for the first time. It covers direct purchases from Anthropic now, and purchases through cloud providers "in the coming months."
- **Expanded eligibility**: the discount now extends to state, local, tribal, and territorial (SLTT) governments, not just the federal government.
- **No minimum spend**: agencies don't have to commit to buying a set amount up front — they pay only for what they use.
- **New implementation program**: Anthropic is launching a dedicated program, expected "in the coming weeks," to help agencies deploy Claude and get value from it, based on practices Anthropic already uses with private-sector customers.

Anthropic estimates the discounts will save the government about $1 billion. The company also reports usage is growing fast: about 500 U.S. federal, state, and local organizations now use Claude; weekly active users of Claude for Government are up roughly 8x since February; and federal civilian usage is up about 20x over the past year.

## Why it matters

Government agencies typically work with tight budgets and slow procurement processes, which can make it hard to try new software without a large upfront commitment. Removing the minimum-spend requirement and widening the discount to every product (not just one) lowers the barrier for an agency to start using Claude, or to expand from a pilot into real use. The expansion to state, local, tribal, and territorial governments broadens who can get the discount — previously it was narrower.

Anthropic shared several concrete examples of agencies already using Claude, which show the kind of work this deal is meant to make more affordable:

- **Utah**: gave Claude Code to 34 developers across 10 state agencies; in a four-week pilot, 80% had a working setup within 30 minutes and 71% reported saving 20+ hours.
- A state CIO's team responded to a ransomware attack in 8 minutes, versus a usual 2–3 days.
- A federal security team used Claude Mythos to find and patch a vulnerability — missed by a human penetration test the month before — in about two days.
- A state IT developer migrated nearly 900 API methods to a new framework in two days, a task originally scoped at one month.

## How to use it

- Agencies can buy Claude at this discounted rate through GSA's Multiple Award Schedule now.
- Agencies that want to build their own tools can access Claude through Amazon Bedrock (FedRAMP High; select models authorized for DoD IL4/IL5 in AWS GovCloud) or Google Cloud Vertex AI (FedRAMP High).
- Claude for Government, generally available since September 30, 2026, offers near feature parity with Anthropic's commercial products at FedRAMP High authorization. Claude Enterprise is separately pursuing FedRAMP Moderate authorization.
- Interested agencies can contact Anthropic's Public Sector team via the link on Anthropic's government solutions page.
