## What changed

To mark Teachers' Day, OpenAI published an update about teaching with AI, covering two things:

- **AI for Educators in Brazilian Portuguese**: OpenAI's free course for teachers, "AI for Educators," is now available in Brazilian Portuguese on OpenAI Academy, OpenAI's free online learning platform. It's aimed at primary, secondary, and higher-education teachers, and covers using AI to plan lessons, build activities and assessments, and draft communications — while the teacher stays in charge of actual teaching decisions.
- **Study Mode in ChatGPT**: OpenAI highlighted this existing learning feature, which walks a student through a topic one step at a time using questions, hints, explanations, and checks for understanding, instead of just giving the answer outright. OpenAI says it built Study Mode with input from teachers, scientists, and pedagogy (teaching-method) experts.

## Why it matters

Many teachers are unsure how to use AI with students without just letting it do the work for them. A free, localized course gives Portuguese-speaking teachers a structured way to learn this, rather than figuring it out alone. Study Mode is aimed at the same concern from the student's side: it's designed to encourage reasoning and active participation instead of letting a student copy a direct answer.

## How to use it

- **Study Mode**: In ChatGPT, open the tools menu and select **Study**, then start a session on the topic you want to work through. It will ask guiding questions one at a time instead of immediately explaining the full answer.
- Teachers can try Study Mode themselves first, on a subject they already know well, to judge whether the questions actually promote reasoning and whether the explanations match their class's level, before using it with students.
- **AI for Educators (Brazilian Portuguese)**: Enroll through the course page on OpenAI Academy.
