## What changed

OpenAI announced, on October 28, 2026, that eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers can claim free credits for the OpenAI API (the pay-as-you-go service developers use to call OpenAI's models from their own code). This is on top of the regular ChatGPT subscription — subscribers get API credits as an extra benefit, at no extra charge.

The credit amount depends on the plan:

- **Pro plans**: $100, $200, or $500 per month, depending on the specific Pro tier.
- **Business plans**: $20 per paid Standard seat or $100 per paid Premium seat, pooled together, up to $500 per workspace per month.

To activate the benefit, a subscriber links an API organization they own to their ChatGPT subscription. OpenAI says credits are claimed from ChatGPT billing settings.

## Why it matters

Normally, using the OpenAI API costs money separately from a ChatGPT subscription — you pay per request, with no connection between the two. This change means people already paying for ChatGPT Pro or Business get a recurring allowance to build their own apps and agents with the API, instead of paying twice. For a developer or small team already on one of these plans, this can cover meaningful API usage (prototyping, a side project, or testing an integration) without a separate bill, at least up to the monthly cap.

## How to use it

1. Go to ChatGPT billing settings to claim the credits: [chatgpt.com/settings/billing](https://chatgpt.com/settings/billing).
2. Use the OpenAI Developers plugin in ChatGPT or Codex to create an API key for an organization you own, and link that organization to activate the benefit: [chatgpt.com/plugins/openai-developers](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/openai-developers?open_in_app).
3. Credits are capped by plan as listed above, and are granted monthly — check OpenAI's help article for exact eligibility and renewal details.
