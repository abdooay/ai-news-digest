## What changed

OpenAI announced an independent advisory group of mathematicians to advise it on AI's growing role in mathematics research. The nine members are: François Charton (ENS-PSL), Camillo De Lellis (IAS, GSSI), Timothy Gowers (Collège de France, Cambridge), Martin Hairer (EPFL, Imperial College London), Nikhil Srivastava (Berkeley, Simons Institute), Ulrike Tillmann (Oxford, INI), Ravi Vakil (Stanford), Edward Witten (IAS), and Melanie Matchett Wood (Harvard).

The group's job is to advise on how OpenAI reviews and communicates mathematical results claimed by its models — checking their significance and making sure claims meet academic standards before they're publicized — and to advise on how AI tools can support math research and education more broadly.

OpenAI says the group is independent by design: members are unpaid, exercise their own judgment, can challenge OpenAI's decisions, and remain free to publicly comment on or criticize OpenAI's mathematical work.

This follows OpenAI's announcement in September 2026 that one of its internal models had resolved the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem along with more than 100 other open math problems, which drew scrutiny from mathematicians about how such claims should be verified and communicated.

## Why it matters

When a company claims its own AI model solved a hard, well-known math problem, that claim needs independent checking before the wider world can trust it — a company grading its own homework isn't convincing on its own. An advisory group made of respected mathematicians who aren't paid by OpenAI, and who say they're free to publicly disagree with it, is a step toward outside verification for these kinds of claims, similar in spirit to Anthropic's recent moves to bring in independent evaluators for its own models.

## How to use it

This is a governance change at OpenAI, not a product or tool. There's nothing to install or try. It mainly matters if you follow claims about AI solving math problems: watch for this group's involvement in how such claims get reviewed and communicated going forward.
