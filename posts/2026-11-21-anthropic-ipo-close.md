## What changed

Anthropic announced it has closed its initial public offering (IPO — the process of selling shares to the public for the first time) and a separate, private share sale that happened alongside it.

In the IPO, Anthropic sold 1,127,000,000 shares of Class A common stock at $83.00 each. That included 147,000,000 extra shares that the underwriters (the banks managing the sale) had an option to buy, which they used in full. Of the shares sold, 1,103,351,354 came from Anthropic itself and 23,648,646 came from existing shareholders selling some of their stock. Anthropic received no money from the shares sold by those existing shareholders. The IPO raised about $93.5 billion before fees, and Anthropic's own share of that was about $91.6 billion.

The stock started trading on November 19, 2026, on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol "ANT." The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) declared the paperwork for the sale effective on November 18, 2026.

Alongside the IPO, Anthropic privately sold 313,253,007 shares of a different stock class, Class N common stock, also at $83.00 each. The buyers were Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), Broadcom, Google, Micron Technology, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC Partners. This private sale raised about $26.0 billion and was not registered with the SEC, since it wasn't offered to the public.

Combined, the IPO and the private sale raised about $119.5 billion before fees and expenses. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led the group of banks that managed the offering.

## Why it matters

This gives Anthropic a large amount of cash to fund its operations, including the computing power it needs to train and run its Claude models. The private placement is notable because the buyers are mostly chip and cloud companies — AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and TSMC Partners — all of whom make or supply the hardware AI companies depend on. That suggests those companies see a strategic reason to hold a stake in Anthropic directly, beyond a normal public-market investment.

For everyday users of Claude or Claude Code, this announcement doesn't change anything about the product. It mainly affects how Anthropic is funded and who its financial stakeholders are.
