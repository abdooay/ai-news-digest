## What changed

Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, priced its initial public offering (IPO) — the process of selling shares of a company to the public for the first time. The announcement, published November 18, 2026, gives these facts:

- **Price:** $83.00 per share of Class A common stock.
- **Shares offered:** 980,000,000 Class A shares in total. Of those, 956,351,354 are being sold by Anthropic itself, and 23,648,646 are being sold by existing shareholders ("selling stockholders").
- **Underwriter option:** Anthropic gave its underwriters (the banks running the sale) a 30-day option to buy up to 147,000,000 more shares.
- **Ticker and exchange:** The shares will trade on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT".
- **Timing:** The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) declared Anthropic's registration statement effective on November 18, 2026. Trading was expected to begin November 19, 2026, with the sale expected to close (payment and delivery of shares complete) on November 20, 2026, subject to standard closing conditions.
- **Underwriters named in the announcement:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC.
- The announcement states that Anthropic "will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders" — meaning only the money from Anthropic's own 956,351,354 shares goes to the company, not the portion sold by existing shareholders.

The announcement does not state a company valuation, total proceeds, or what Anthropic plans to do with the money it raises.

## Why it matters

This is a major financial and structural milestone: Anthropic is moving from a private company, funded by investors like any startup, to a publicly traded one, whose shares anyone can buy or sell on a stock exchange. For everyday users and developers, going public doesn't change how Claude or Claude Code work. But it does mean Anthropic will face new disclosure requirements (regular public financial reports) and new public-market pressures that can shape its roadmap and pricing decisions over time. It's also one of the largest AI-sector IPOs to date, going by the number of shares and price listed.

## How to use it

There's nothing to install or configure — this doesn't change Claude's products or APIs. If you're interested in the financial details, Anthropic's own investor relations materials (referenced in the official announcement) are the authoritative source for the prospectus and any follow-up filings.
