## What changed

OpenAI Academy, OpenAI's free program for teaching practical AI skills, marked two years since it launched in September 2024. OpenAI says the program has run more than 250 events and reached over 4 million people, through self-paced online courses, practical guides, and in-person workshops called "AI Skills Jams." Learners can earn course badges by passing assessments. One example event cited is an AI Skills Jam for K-12 educators that brought together more than 1,600 teachers, administrators, and district leaders across eight U.S. cities.

Alongside the anniversary, OpenAI announced a new Community Trainer Program. It lets outside organizations — schools, workforce development groups, small-business networks, and community organizations — get trained to teach OpenAI Academy material themselves, in their own communities, rather than relying only on OpenAI-run events. This follows OpenAI's recent introduction of role-based learning paths for developers, knowledge workers, educators, leaders, and college students (covered in an earlier digest post).

## Why it matters

Free AI training programs run directly by an AI company are one way skills spread, but they're limited by how many events that one company can run itself. Recruiting and training outside organizations to teach the material is a way to scale that reach well beyond what OpenAI's own staff could do directly — similar to a "train the trainer" model used in other education and public-health programs.

## How to use it

- OpenAI Academy's courses and guides are free and available online for anyone who wants to build practical AI skills.
- If you run a school, workforce program, small-business network, or community group, the new Community Trainer Program is the path to becoming a certified local trainer of OpenAI Academy material — check the OpenAI Academy site for how to apply.
