## What changed

OpenAI announced ["Funding grants for new research into AI and teen development"](https://openai.com/index/teen-development-research-grants/) on September 8, 2026. The company is setting aside $5 million to fund independent research on how generative AI affects the lives and development of teenagers aged 13 to 17. Individual grants can be as large as $1 million, and at most 10% of any grant can go toward overhead costs rather than the research itself.

To apply, researchers must be at least 18 years old and affiliated with a research institution, or otherwise have relevant expertise in child development, AI's effects on people, or a related field. OpenAI says it will prioritize nonprofit organizations and academic researchers over for-profit companies.

The program is looking for research on: how teens actually use generative AI and how different patterns of use relate to their development; effects on emotional and social development, including resilience, identity, and relationships; how these effects differ across demographics, cultures, and economic backgrounds; and technical safeguards or age-appropriate design choices. OpenAI says it welcomes interdisciplinary proposals combining psychology, public health, sociology, and AI safety research.

Applications are open through October 6, 2026, with decisions expected by November 13, 2026.

## Why it matters

OpenAI frames this as building an evidence base to guide its own product decisions and inform public policy on AI and minors, an area under increasing regulatory and public scrutiny. For researchers in child development, psychology, or AI safety, this is a concrete funding opportunity. For everyone else, it's a signal that OpenAI expects more scrutiny — and more product changes — around how teenagers use its tools, though this announcement itself doesn't change anything about how ChatGPT works today.

## How to use it

- If you're a researcher who studies child or adolescent development, AI's societal effects, or a related field, and you want to apply: OpenAI's post is the announcement, but check OpenAI's official grants page (linked from the announcement) for the actual application form and full eligibility rules before applying.
- Note the deadline: applications close October 6, 2026.
