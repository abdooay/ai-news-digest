## What changed

OpenAI published "Reimagining advertising with AI" on September 16, 2026, introducing Sponsored Agents: a new ad format inside ChatGPT. Instead of sending a user to an advertiser's website, clicking certain ads opens a branded conversation with that business's AI agent, right inside ChatGPT. The sponsored conversation is clearly labeled and kept separate from both ChatGPT's own answers and the user's original conversation.

The format is being tested with a small number of advertisers in the US. The first two are:

- **Wayfair**, for furniture shopping — OpenAI says Wayfair is using the pilot to test whether a conversation can help shoppers make more informed decisions, with guardrails around product accuracy, transparency, and handoff to human customer service.
- **Angi**, for home services — a homeowner can describe a project to an Angi-branded agent, and when the job needs a professional, the agent can hand off into Angi's existing flow for connecting with local pros.

Alongside Sponsored Agents, OpenAI also added its first CRM and e-commerce advertiser integrations, with HubSpot and Shopify. A Shopify ChatGPT Ads app is expected to roll out more broadly on September 23, 2026.

## Why it matters

This turns some ChatGPT ads into a two-way conversation rather than a link out to a website. For users, it means an ad can be followed up with questions before deciding whether to visit the business's site. For businesses, it's a new advertising channel inside ChatGPT, separate from paying for search or display ads. It's also a sign that OpenAI is building out ad infrastructure (like the HubSpot and Shopify integrations) beyond just the sponsored-agent pilot itself.

## How to use it

- This is not yet a general feature — it's a limited pilot with select US advertisers, so most ChatGPT users won't see it yet.
- Businesses interested in advertising on ChatGPT, including trying Sponsored Agents, can apply through OpenAI's advertiser program.
- Businesses already using HubSpot or Shopify can look out for the new ad integrations, with the Shopify app expanding internationally on September 23, 2026.
