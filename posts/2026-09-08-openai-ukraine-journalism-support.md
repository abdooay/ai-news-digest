## What changed

On September 7, 2026, OpenAI, WAN-IFRA (the World Association of News Publishers), and AIRPPU (the Association of Independent Regional Press Publishers of Ukraine) announced a joint program to help Ukrainian news organizations adopt AI. Details are in [OpenAI's announcement](https://openai.com/index/supporting-independent-journalism-in-ukraine).

The program has two parts:

- **Newsroom AI Masterclass Series** — training sessions where international experts share practical knowledge on using AI for editorial work, audience engagement, product development, revenue, and organizational change. This started on August 5, 2026.
- **Newsroom AI Catalyst** — more hands-on support for ten Ukrainian news organizations selected by AIRPPU, helping them pick specific AI use cases, build an implementation plan, and pilot AI tools. This launches September 17, 2026.

All participating organizations also get credits toward OpenAI's API so they can build custom tools for their newsrooms. No specific funding amount was disclosed.

## Why it matters

This is a partnership and training program, not a new AI model or feature — its relevance is mostly to people working in media, especially independent Ukrainian publishers operating during ongoing conflict, where resources for adopting new technology are limited. It's also one more example of OpenAI building relationships with news organizations, following similar earlier partnerships mentioned in the announcement.

## How to use it

If you work at an independent Ukrainian regional or local news organization, participation in the Newsroom AI Catalyst is arranged through AIRPPU. Program and application details are posted on [WAN-IFRA's event page](https://wan-ifra.org/events/newsroom-ai-catalyst-ukraine/).
