## What changed

On October 14, 2026, Anthropic announced it has opened a new office in Singapore — its fifth in the Asia-Pacific region — and is now open and hiring there. Dale Finlay, Anthropic's General Manager for ASEAN, said the company is "committed to Singapore for the long term."

Alongside the office opening, Anthropic signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information, signed by the Ministry's Permanent Secretary Kai Fong Chng and Anthropic co-founder Ben Mann at Singapore International Cyber Week. The MOU covers:

- Cooperation on AI safety, security, and model evaluation.
- Research into how AI is being adopted and its effects.
- Responsible AI adoption in both public and private sectors.

Planned activities include technical exchanges between Anthropic and Singapore's AI safety and security institutions (on model evaluation, testing standards, and incident response), help for small and medium businesses adopting AI, support for building AI skills among public officers, and sharing of research and data, including from the Anthropic Economic Index.

This follows earlier work: Anthropic joined Singapore's Generative AI Evaluation Sandbox in 2023 and later worked on a red-teaming project covering English, Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil.

Anthropic also shared some numbers on existing Singapore usage: Singapore ranks second out of 121 countries for Claude.ai usage relative to population size. GovTech Singapore says more than 1,500 of its engineers and technical staff use Claude Code, and the Cyber Security Agency of Singapore is exploring Claude for cyber defense. Banks DBS and GIC use Claude in their Singapore operations. Grab's "BriX" sales assistant, built on Claude, reportedly saves its sales teams about 40,000 hours per quarter. Sea's Shopee uses Claude for customer service and internal coding work.

## Why it matters

This is mainly a business-expansion and government-relations story rather than a product change — no new Claude features ship with it. The concrete takeaway for developers in Singapore (or building for Singapore-based customers) is that Anthropic now has a local office and a formal channel with the Singapore government for AI safety standards and testing, which could shape local compliance requirements over time.

## How to use it

- No action needed for most users — this doesn't change any Claude product or API.
- If your company is in Singapore and wants to adopt Claude, Anthropic's announcement mentions upcoming support specifically aimed at small and medium-sized businesses.
