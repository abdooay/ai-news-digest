## What changed

On July 28, 2026, OpenAI announced the Student Collective, a program for undergraduate students called "Campus Leads." Campus Leads work in pairs at their school to run workshops, "Studio Hours" (open help sessions), and project showcases that teach other students how to use ChatGPT and Codex (OpenAI's coding tool).

In exchange, OpenAI gives Campus Leads training, funding, product credits, a stipend, swag, and access to a global community of other Campus Leads. OpenAI says applicants don't need prior experience with Codex or an existing portfolio of AI projects — the company says it's looking for people who are curious and good at bringing others together.

Applications close August 10, 2026 at 11:59pm Pacific time. OpenAI says it will review applications and invite selected candidates to interview in August.

## Why it matters

This is a recruitment and outreach program, not a product or API change — it doesn't affect how you use ChatGPT, Codex, or the OpenAI API today. It matters mainly if you're an undergraduate interested in leading AI-related activities on your campus, since it comes with paid compensation (a stipend) rather than being purely volunteer.

## How to use it

- Apply, or read the full program details, at [openai.com/student-collective](https://openai.com/student-collective/).
- Applications are due by August 10, 2026, 11:59pm PT.
- The role is for pairs of undergraduate Campus Leads per school; no Codex experience or project portfolio is required to apply.
