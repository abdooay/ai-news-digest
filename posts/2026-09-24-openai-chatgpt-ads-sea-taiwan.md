## What changed

OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Ads — advertisements shown inside ChatGPT — to seven more markets: Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, and Taiwan. Combined with earlier launches elsewhere in the Asia-Pacific region, ChatGPT Ads are now live in more than 60 countries.

OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate (a projection of yearly revenue based on current pace) in under 200 days after launch, with tens of thousands of advertisers now using it. Ads only appear for users on ChatGPT's free and basic (lower, mostly free-of-charge) subscription tiers — paid subscription tiers stay ad-free. OpenAI says ads are always clearly labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT's actual answers, that advertising doesn't influence what ChatGPT says, and that user conversations stay private from advertisers. Businesses can buy ads through direct agency partnerships, OpenAI's own Ads Solutions team, technology partners, or a self-service Ads Manager for eligible companies.

## Why it matters

This is mainly relevant to advertisers and to users on ChatGPT's free or basic tiers in the newly added countries, who will start seeing ads if they haven't already. It also shows how OpenAI is funding free access to ChatGPT — through ads on lower tiers rather than through subscriptions alone — a business model already familiar from search engines and social media, now applied to a conversational AI product.

## How to use it

- If you're a user on a free or basic ChatGPT plan in one of the newly added countries, you may start seeing labeled ads; switching to a paid plan removes them.
- If you're a business that wants to advertise in ChatGPT, apply through OpenAI's Ads Solutions team, an ad agency partner, or the self-service Ads Manager if your company is eligible.
